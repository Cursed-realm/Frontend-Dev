Welcome to Japan

Explore the Land of the Rising Sun, where ancient traditions meet modern innovation. From bustling cities to serene temples, Japan offers a unique blend of culture, history, and natural beauty.

About the Destination

Japan is an island country in East Asia. Located in the Pacific Ocean off the northeast coast of the Asian mainland, it is bordered to the west by the Sea of Japan and extends from the Sea of Okhotsk in the north to the East China Sea in the south.
Capital: Tokyo
Population: 12.4 crores (2024)
Prime minister: Shigeru Ishiba
Dialing code: +81
Area: 377,974 km²
Currency: Japanese yen (¥)

Culture & Events

Japan boasts a rich cultural heritage, from traditional tea ceremonies and kimono wearing to modern pop culture phenomena like anime and J-pop. Annual events such as the Cherry Blossom Festival in spring and the Gion Matsuri in Kyoto showcase Japan's vibrant traditions and community spirit.
