Box-Sizing Comparison

Understanding the difference between content-box (default) and border-box. Both boxes below have the same specified width (200px), padding (20px), and border (10px), but their total widths differ due to different box-sizing models.

content-box (default)
border-box

content-box (default)

Total width = content + padding + border

Content Area
Content Width: 200px
Padding: 20px × 2 = 40px
Border: 10px × 2 = 20px
Total Width: 260px

border-box

Total width = specified width (includes padding and border)

Content Area
Total Width: 200px
Padding: 20px × 2 (included)
Border: 10px × 2 (included)
Content Width: 140px

Key Differences:

  • content-box: The specified width (200px) applies to the content area only. Padding and border are added to the total width.
  • border-box: The specified width (200px) includes padding and border. The content area shrinks to accommodate them.
  • This is why border-box is often preferred for layouts - it makes sizing more intuitive.
