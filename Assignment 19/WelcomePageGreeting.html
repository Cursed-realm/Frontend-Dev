✨ Welcome Page Greeting Features

  • ⏰ Time-based Greeting: The greeting changes based on time of day (Morning 5am-12pm, Afternoon 12pm-6pm, Evening 6pm-5am)
  • 💡 Change Greeting Button: Displays a random motivational quote from a curated list
  • 👁️ Toggle Welcome Message: Hide or show the welcome message with smooth animation
  • 🔔 Click Alert: Click on the greeting text to see an alert with the current greeting message
  • 🎨 Beautiful Styling: Gradient background, smooth animations, and hover effects

🎯 How to Test

  • Refresh the page to see the time-based greeting update
  • Click the greeting text to trigger an alert
  • Click "Change Greeting" to see a random motivational quote
  • Click "Hide Welcome Message" to toggle the welcome text
  • Hover over buttons to see interactive effects
