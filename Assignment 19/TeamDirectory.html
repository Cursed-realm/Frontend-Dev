👥 Team Members Directory

Interactive organizational structure with hierarchical relationships and DOM traversal.

Features

1️⃣ Click Manager → Highlight Direct Reports

Click on an employee with a manager icon (👔) to highlight them and their direct reports using blue highlighting.

2️⃣ Hover Employee → Show Contact Info

Hover over any employee card to reveal their email and phone number stored in hidden contact elements.

3️⃣ Click Department → Change Member Background

Click the "Change BG" button in a department header to randomly change the background color of all members using .children().

4️⃣ Select Random Employee → Highlight Siblings

Click the "Select" button on any employee to highlight all sibling employees (same department) with orange highlighting.

5️⃣ Collapse/Expand Team

Click the department name or toggle arrow to collapse/expand the team using .parent() and .find() DOM traversal methods.

Technical Notes:
  • Uses DOM traversal: .next(), .parent(), .find(), .children(), .siblings() patterns
  • Department colors change dynamically
  • Contact info shown on hover via display toggle
  • Managers indicated with 👔 icon
